Privacy Policy

Linguanest · Last updated October 4, 2026

Linguanest helps you look up, translate, save, and practice vocabulary. This page explains what information the app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form). If you sign in with Apple, we receive Apple's anonymous user identifier and the email address you choose to share, which may be a private relay address.
  • Your content: the words and phrases you save, their translations, the dates you saved them, and your language settings.
  • Practice results: how many times you answered each saved word correctly or incorrectly in the games.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your saved words and progress available on your devices, choose which words to practice next, and translate text you ask us to translate. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • DeepL receives the text you ask to translate, so it can return a translation. DeepL's privacy policy applies to that text.
  • Our hosting providers store and serve the data: Render runs the service, and Turso (on Amazon Web Services in Ireland) stores the database.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple. If you use the dictation feature, speech recognition is performed by Apple's Speech framework, and we never receive or store your audio.

Cookies

The website uses a single cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account details, saved words, and practice results are permanently deleted, and, if you signed in with Apple, we revoke the connection to your Apple ID.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app or on the website under Settings → Delete account.
  • Edit or delete individual words from your lists, or reset your practice statistics.
  • You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your data by emailing us at the address below.

Children

Linguanest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
