Common questions

The app is slow to open or sign in.
The server can go to sleep when it hasn't been used for a while, and the first request may take up to a minute to wake it. After that it's fast.

How do I choose the language I'm learning?
Open Settings and set I'm learning and Translate into. You can also set how many words each game uses.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, saved words, and practice results. You can also do this on the website under Settings, or email us.

I forgot my password.
Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll help you regain access.
