## Description (4000)
Linguanest is a personal vocabulary notebook for anyone learning a language.

Come across a word or sentence you don't know while reading? Type it in (or dictate it), see the translation, listen to the pronunciation, and save it to your list in one tap.

SAVE & ORGANIZE
• Translate words or whole sentences between German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch
• Your words are saved by day, so it's easy to see what you picked up and when
• Add many words at once with simple "word : translation" lines
• Edit or delete anything, and export your lists as a file

HEAR IT
• Listen to any word or translation read aloud in the right language
• Dictate instead of typing

PRACTICE
• Games built from your own saved words: multiple choice or type-it, in both directions, with hints from easy to hard
• Words you miss and brand-new words come up more often, so practice goes where it helps most
• Typing is forgiving about capital letters, spaces, and hyphens

TRACK PROGRESS
• See which words give you the most trouble, with accuracy for each

Sign in with email or Sign in with Apple. Your account and all your data can be deleted at any time from Settings.

## App Review notes
Sign-up is open: reviewers can create an account with any email and an 8+ character password, or use Sign in with Apple. No demo account is required.

The backend runs on a free hosting plan that sleeps when idle, so the very first request after a quiet period can take up to a minute. If the login screen seems to hang at first, please wait and retry.

To try the main features after signing in: tap Today, type a word (e.g. "Haus"), tap Translate, then Save. Open Games after saving at least 4 words (the Lists tab has a "+" button to add several words at once, e.g. "Hund : dog").

## App Privacy answers ("nutrition labels")
Tracking: **No** — we do not track users.

Data collected, all **linked to the user**, used only for **App Functionality**, and **not used for tracking**:
- Contact Info → **Email Address**
- User Content → **Other User Content** (saved words, translations, and practice results)
- Identifiers → **User ID**

Not collected: location, contacts, photos/videos, audio (dictation uses Apple's on-device/Apple speech recognition; the app never receives the audio), browsing/search history, purchases, diagnostics, advertising data.

Note for the questionnaire: text you translate is sent to DeepL to be translated; that's covered under "Other User Content".
